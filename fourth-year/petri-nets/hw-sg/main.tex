\section*{Solution}
In the network $N = \langle P, T, F \rangle$ Given that $N$ is live, safe and connected, we will show that $G$ - a graph constructed from $N$ is strongly connected. \\
Let $v \in V(G)$, and $N$ bounded with $m \in \mathbf{N}$. We need to show that the set $S$ of vertices that reeach $v$ is equal to $V(G)$. Assume for the sake of contradiction it's not. \\ 
Since $N$ is connected, there are edges from $S$ to $U=V(G)\setminus S$. Take $u \in U$ such that there is an edge from $s \in S$ to $u$. \\
Either $u \in P, s \in T$ or $u \in T, s \in P$. Using liveness of $N$ take an initial marking $M$ and a sequence of transitions $\sigma$ fireable from $M$ such that $u$ or $s$ (depending on which is a transition) appear $m+1$ times. 
Since there are no edges from $U$ to $S$, all transitions in $U$ can never activate transitions in $S$, therefore $\sigma$ will still be a firable sequence if we remove all transitions from $S$. Call this new sequence $\tilde \sigma$ \\ 
Observe that $M \firee {\tilde \sigma} $ has $m+1$ tokens in $u$ or $s$ (depending on which is a place).\\
If $u$ was a place, then we have fired $s$ $m+1$ times, so in total $m+1$ tokens appeared in $u$. None of them could have left $u$, since all transitions are in $U$.\\
If $s$ was a place, then there must be $m+1$ tokens in $s$ after firing $\tilde \sigma$ because we were supposed to fire $u$ $m+1$ times and we didn't.
This contradicts boundness of $N$ on $M$, so $G$ is strongly connected.
